App Store Connect (metadata) — Guideline 3.1.2(c) and 2.3.2
------------------------------------------------------------
1. App Privacy: Privacy Policy field = same URL as EXPO_PUBLIC_PRIVACY_POLICY_URL.
2. EULA: Either upload a custom EULA in App Information, OR use Apple’s standard EULA and add Apple’s Terms of Use link to the App Description (per Apple’s instructions).
3. App Description: Add one clear sentence that full Pro features require a paid subscription (monthly/annual), e.g.:
   “Full access to Pro features requires an active subscription (monthly or annual); optional free preview may be available. See in-app purchase details.”
4. Promoted In-App Purchases / win-back (Guideline 2.3.2): Replace duplicate promotional images. Use unique, non-screenshot artwork per product (Apple’s current size requirements in ASC).
